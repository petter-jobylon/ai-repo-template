## Design Specificity Verdict
Branded, not yet owned (about 6/10). Specific: roster with calendar absence, "Next: Mikael", Linear panels, Jobylon lockup with Cooper BT title. Interchangeable: the ring, name, CTA focus card and the three question cards. Nothing reflects Trustcruit beyond the title.
Detector: 3 warnings, all false positives (clamp() padding on .focus and .quote read as zero; flat-type-hierarchy reported sizes the render does not have: h1 30px, speaker h2 54px, panel h2 14px, body 16px).

## Overall Impression
Works as a stand-up tool: time, speaker and next person read from across a room. The weakness is weighting: static questions get the most room while live per-speaker data and total-time pressure get the least.

## What's Working
1. The focus card: tabular clock in a ring, 54px name, "Next: Mikael" button as one glanceable unit.
2. One layout, three jobs: before, during and after reuse the same frame.
3. Brand fidelity and theming: Cooper BT only for title and quote, contrast passes (lowest 4.84:1), 2px offset focus rings in both themes.

## Priority Issues
1. [P1] Static questions outweigh live data; right column looks empty. Fix: compress the questions into one strip, give the height to the tickets panel, raise row text to about 17px. Command: layout.
2. [P1] Over-time and total-time pressure are under-signalled. Total elapsed is a 14px pill; red and over levels look identical; Next does not change. Fix: 20px+ total-time readout, stronger primary button in red and over, a text line such as "Over by 0:12, hand over". Command: bolder.
3. [P2] Too many controls (8 options during), a loud red End button, no undo for a mis-advance. Fix: ghost End that turns red on confirm; short Undo on advance via the existing toast. Command: distill.
4. [P2] Roster too small for whole-meeting state; 12px order badges overlap avatars; 6+ people wrap. Fix: order in the card text, 16px state text, single shrinking row. Command: adapt.
5. [P3] The finish does not land: early-finish ring reads as a failed timer, triage is next, "Skipped" is judgmental, some quotes generic. Fix: ok-colored full ring, "Done in 12:40, 2:20 under time", "Not reached", team-specific quotes. Command: delight.

## Persona Red Flags
Alex: no undo for accidental N; hint hidden under 800px height and on touch; no key for Start, End or extend; right arrow advances while a card has focus.
Casey: about 1700px tall at 390x844, roster and Next never on screen together; header stacks into several rows; Next turns navy after a tap (sticky hover on touch); ticket titles truncate to ids.
Sam: contrast and focus rings pass, live region announces every change; roster labels do not state current state; order and done badges have no text alternative; clock has no accessible name.
