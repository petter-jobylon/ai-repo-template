# Decisions

One line per non-obvious choice, newest at the bottom: `[YYYY-MM-DD] <decision>: <why>`, at most 300 characters. Escalations: `[YYYY-MM-DD] ESCALATED: <question> -> <who>`, then `[YYYY-MM-DD] RESOLVED <escalated date>: <answer>`. Rules for what goes here and what becomes an ADR: `CLAUDE.md` § Decisions. Completed months move to `docs/decisions/YYYY-MM.md`.
